


 Minutes of the Special GEnie Forth RT Conference with George Nicol,
 president of Silicon Composers. Dr. Nicol discussed his company's
 SBC32 single board computer based on the 32 bit Forth engine created
 at Johns-Hopkins Advanced Physics Lab.
