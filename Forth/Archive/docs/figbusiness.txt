<[John] JDHALL> This is a little long, but comes at 3 sec intervals..
<[John] JDHALL> My recollections.........
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> Forth Interest Group was organized in 1978 and one of the
<[John] JDHALL> first things that was done was the formation of Forth
<[John] JDHALL> Implementation Team lead by Bill Ragsdale to build figForth
<[John] JDHALL> and put it in the public domain.  Because figForth was
<[John] JDHALL> implemented on many microprocessors based on a single
<[John] JDHALL> model and released with complete source listings,  it
<[John] JDHALL> became the defacto standard of Forth.  The listing were
<[John] JDHALL> made available for $15 and were extremely popular.  To
<[John] JDHALL> encourage extensions and modifications to Forth, the same
<[John] JDHALL> people started Forth Dimensions, FORML and the Forth
<[John] JDHALL> Standards Team.  Many of these same people wrote or
<[John] JDHALL> encouraged others to write articles for Byte and the famous
<[John] JDHALL> Byte Forth issue was published in Oct of 1980.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> Money was coming in faster than new uses for it could be
<[John] JDHALL> found and sales taxes were collected but no formal
<[John] JDHALL> organization existed to pay the sales tax and avoid any
<[John] JDHALL> income tax.  At this time, FIG was only a loose confederation
<[John] JDHALL> of interested Forth users that were spread around the world,
<[John] JDHALL> so a California non-profit corporation, Forth Interest
<[John] JDHALL> Group,Inc., was formed to centralize the contacts, establish a
<[John] JDHALL> central distribution point for Forth information and establish
<[John] JDHALL> the formal function of publishing Forth Dimensions and the
<[John] JDHALL> FORML proceedings and the distribution of these and the
<[John] JDHALL> figForth listings.  No great thought was given to the other
<[John] JDHALL> reasons for having a corporation.  It was primarily to
<[John] JDHALL> centralize the loose confederation of people and ideas and
<[John] JDHALL> distribute contributed literature.  FIG was guided by a Board
<[John] JDHALL> of Directors consisting of Bill Ragsdale, Kim Harris, John
<[John] JDHALL> James, Dave Kilbridge and Dave Boulton.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> The corporation wasn't the answer to the problems of
<[John] JDHALL> organization, it took people to make ideas happen.
<[John] JDHALL> Organization ideas were and still are plentiful, people to
<[John] JDHALL> implement the ideas were not.  From the beginning
<[John] JDHALL> business meetings were held one evening a month on the
<[John] JDHALL> Tuesday before the 4th Saturday of the month.  Before the
<[John] JDHALL> corporation, FIG and the Silicon Valley chapter were the
<[John] JDHALL> same and the planning for the Sat. meeting was
<[John] JDHALL> finalized on the previous Tue.   As the corporation was
<[John] JDHALL> formed, the corporation business meeting began to take over
<[John] JDHALL> the topics of the Tue. meeting until finally the Silicon Valley
<[John] JDHALL> chapter planning was split off to another night.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> Since actions took more time than was available at the
<[John] JDHALL> business meetings, committees were formed to plan and
<[John] JDHALL> guide activities as existing or new functions were needed.
<[John] JDHALL> FORML and Forth Dimensions needed guidance.  FORML split
<[John] JDHALL> into a conference and a convention.  People around the
<[John] JDHALL> world were forming group meetings and wanted to be kept
<[John] JDHALL> informed, so a chapter committee was formed.  Literature
<[John] JDHALL> needed to be published and new books and publications
<[John] JDHALL> needed to be reviewed. A publication committee was formed.
<[John] JDHALL> Direct communication with the Forth community was
<[John] JDHALL> needed, so a connection to GEnie was established and a
<[John] JDHALL> committee of sysops was formed   All of the literature
<[John] JDHALL> needed to be distributed and orders needed to be filled, so
<[John] JDHALL> an outside organization was hired.  Money was being
<[John] JDHALL> received and distributed on a daily basis and this was more
<[John] JDHALL> than the volunteer treasurer could handle.  From the
<[John] JDHALL> beginning, all of the functions of the FIG, Inc were
<[John] JDHALL> handled by a group volunteers with the help a few paid
<[John] JDHALL> people such as the editor of Forth Dimensions and Mountain
<[John] JDHALL> View Press for distribution.  When we saw our way clear
<[John] JDHALL> enough, a management organization was hired to try to tie
<[John] JDHALL> all the office functions together to establish a central office
<[John] JDHALL> for mail distribution and phone under the direct control of
<[John] JDHALL> FIG, Inc.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> We are now at a point now where FIG, Inc, includes
<[John] JDHALL>   1)  Editing, publication and Distribution of Forth Dimensions
<[John] JDHALL>   2)  Production and convening the Annual FORML Conference
<[John] JDHALL>   3)  Publication and distribution of the FORML Proceedings
<[John] JDHALL>   4)  Production and convening the Annual Forth Convention
<[John] JDHALL>   5)  Support and Sponsorship of the GEnie RoundTable
<[John] JDHALL>   6)  Support and organization of approx 50 Chapters
<[John] JDHALL>   7)  Distribution of over 90 publications
<[John] JDHALL>   8)  Production and distribution of a growing disk library
<[John] JDHALL>   9)  Membership enrollment of over 2000 members
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> FIG, Inc is governed by a 7 member Board of Directors:
<[John] JDHALL> Dennis Ruffer, John Hall, Terry Sutton, Mike Elola,
<[John] JDHALL> Robert Smith, Jack Brown, Wil Baden
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> Business is conducted by the Business Group including
<[John] JDHALL> the above and:
<[John] JDHALL> C.H. Ting, Jan Shepherd, Bob Barr, Marlin Ouverson,
<[John] JDHALL> Bill Ragsdale, Robert Reiling, Tom Zimmer and others
<[John] JDHALL> who are interested in the business of FIG
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> The growth of FIG was probably not as chaotic as I am
<[John] JDHALL> remembering it, but the organizing was driven by the needs
<[John] JDHALL> that  had to be filled rather than with a planned structure
<[John] JDHALL> and the organizing was done by those of us who are
<[John] JDHALL> technically competent in Forth but not necessarily in
<[John] JDHALL> organizations.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> Was FIG the answer for Forth?
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> Well, yes, if the question was how do we establish a central
<[John] JDHALL> focal point for Forth.  What we had done is created a
<[John] JDHALL> "passive" organization, given it  ability to reach out to the
<[John] JDHALL> Forth community and made it financially self-supporting as
<[John] JDHALL> long as there were enough people who were interested in
<[John] JDHALL> Forth and willing to support FIG.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> What we had not done was create an "active" environment
<[John] JDHALL> for "encouraging" those who were interested in Forth,
<[John] JDHALL> "instructing" those who were new to Forth, "supporting the
<[John] JDHALL> creativity" of people who were advancing Forth or even
<[John] JDHALL> "establishing the solid technical foundation" and background
<[John] JDHALL> for the growth of Forth use.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> There are probably other things the FIG is not and should
<[John] JDHALL> be.
<[John] JDHALL>
<[John] JDHALL> We have the organization and it has the room for new
<[John] JDHALL> directions.  How do we now make it the organizational
<[John] JDHALL> answer for Forth?
<[John] JDHALL>
