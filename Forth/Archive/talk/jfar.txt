
          The Journal of Forth Application and Research
                                 
JFAR comes out quarterly with reviewed articles, book reports,
technical notes, letters, conference abstracts and special
features. JFAR staff include:

  Lawrence P. Forsley, publisher, University of Rochester
  Dr. James Basile, editor-in-chief, Long Island University
  Dr. Mahlon Kelly, US editor, University of Virginia
  Dr. Hans Nieuwenhuyzen, European editor, University of Utrecht

JFAR is published by the not-for-profit Institute for Applied
Forth Research, Inc. in cooperation with the Long Island
University Faculty of Communication, Computer and Information
Sciences. JFAR is the only peer-reviewed publication on Forth.
Papers are critiqued by an editor and 3 reviewers, familiar with
the area. Reviewers, like authors and readers, come from
companies and universities or are consultants and entrepreneurs.
