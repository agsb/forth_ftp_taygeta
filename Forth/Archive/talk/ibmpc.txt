*** QUEST-4TH-BUGS       12-DEC-84-
  PARENT=IBM-PC               USAGE=422
We have been using the Quest Research Forth to develop commercial
software. While the interprative version runs very nicely, we have
found major problems in the Package Builder Utility which is necessary
to create stand alone applications. The documentation on the PBU is very
difficult to follow and the software is difficult to use. Many weeks of our
developement time have been lost due to bugs in the PBU utility. The software
support at Quest has been terrible. It is almost impossible to get them to a
answer their phone. When they are informed of bugs in their code they have not
been prompt in fixing them or returning phone calls. If anyone plans on using
the Quest package to create software for resale I would strongly advise against
it.
                        Bob
