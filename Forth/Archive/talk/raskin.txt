

 Minutes of the Guest GEnie Forth RoundTable Conference, with special
 guest Jef Raskin, originator of the Apple Mac and the Canon Cat.
  
 The topic was: "What happened to the Cat ?" in reference to the marketing
                failure of the Canon Cat. This initial discussion rapidly
                gave way to a more thorough look at what the interface
                between computer and man should be. Certainly, an area that
                it can be safely argued Jef Raskin is one of the ranking
                authorities of, if not 'the' authority.
