

 Minutes of the Special GEnie Forth RT Conference with Bill Ragsdale,
 president of Dorado Systems. Bill discussed his company's approach
 to 'Targeted Applications in Forth'. Also discussed was various
 Forth models and what Bill sees as a major failure by the X3J14 ANS
 Technical Committee as they construct the new standard.
