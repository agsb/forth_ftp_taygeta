
 Minutes of the Special GEnie Forth RT Conference with Dr. Glen Haydon,
 author of the newly revised "All About Forth" and co-founder of WISC
 Technology. Glen takes issue with standardization as he discusses how
 Forth should be defined by practice, not by committee.
 Glen's Topic: "Forth Common Usage".
