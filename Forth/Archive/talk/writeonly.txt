<[Wil] PRESS19>  hi y all.
<[Wil] PRESS19> Let's stop kidding ourselves. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> forth deserves its reputation as a "write-only: language. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 99 percent of Forth programs published prove this. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> Until Forth improves its reputation, it will be SCORNED..
<[Wil] PRESS19> Tonight I want to investigate the evil forces that cause this....
<[Wil] PRESS19> and discuss six necessary but insufficient rules
<[Wil] PRESS19> for more readable programs. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 1. The stack state must be given for every line. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 2. Formatting must show logical structure. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 3. Use short definitions, short lines, short phrases. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 4. Don't mumble -- your program should pass your spelling 
checker. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 5. Mix upper and lower case -- all lower case is just as bad 
as all upper. ...
<[Wil] PRESS19> 6. Write comments in the English language.
<[Wil] PRESS19> Are there better rules? What else must be done?  ga
