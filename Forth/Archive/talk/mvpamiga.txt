MVP-FORTH, a public domain FORTH on many different systems, is now
available for the Commodore-Amiga.  Mountain View Press (MVP) does sell
this system along with lots of FORTH books, but MVP-FORTH can also be
found at user groups and on bbs'es, but try to get the Amiga specific
documentation!	I am in the process of uploading all of the MVP-FORTH
Amiga modules (source and object) to the Well expecting to have it all
uploading by 13 January 1986.

You can call Mountain View Press at (415)961-4103 (USA) for more info.
