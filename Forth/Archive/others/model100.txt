I called Tandy in Fort Worth in hopes of receiving info about Model 100
internal routines.  They told me they had no such information and suggested I
try the Compuservu Model-100 SIG. Ugh.
  The November '83 issue of the TRS-80 Microcomputer News contains a summary
of a number of ROM routines (LCD, Keyboard, Printer, Sys/1/Modem,Casette &
RAM files).  It does not, however, say anything about how to pass parameters
to these routines.  The issue should be available at most Tandy Computer Ctrs.

*** 100-FORTH-INTERNALS   5-FEB-85
  PARENT=TANDY-SUPPORT        USAGE=220
THE REFERENCED FORTH FOR THE 100 IS A WARMED OVER MVP FORTH WITH ABSOLUTELY
NO HARD STORAGE AND ONLY 8 BLOCK BUFFERS IN MEMORY. IT IS INADEQUATE FOR
ANY SERIOUS PURPOSES, EXCEPT POSSIBLY LEARNING SOMETHING ABOUT FORTH. THE
DOCUMENTATION ONLY TELLS YOU HOW TO LOAD THE TAPE. IT IS ALSO SLOW. AS TO 100
INTERNALS, THE CALLS ARE PROPRIETARY TO MICROSOFT, AND VERY, VERY HARD
TO COME BY UNLESS YOU FIND STUFF BY THE BLACKMARKET AND BACK ALLEY.
